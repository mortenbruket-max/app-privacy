Simple Stud Finder: Privacy Policy

Publisher: EZNO Apps (Simple Tools). Effective 6 September 2026.

Local measurements

Magnetometer readings are processed locally on your phone. We do not upload readings, collect measurement history, run ads or include analytics. No account with us is required. The app does not access your camera, microphone or location. Vibration provides detection feedback if enabled.

Local storage

Sensitivity and vibration settings, the trial start time and a purchase cache are stored locally. Android backup is disabled. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes this local data. Google Play can restore an eligible previous purchase.

Trial and purchases

The free trial lasts 48 hours from first launch. There is no automatic charge or subscription. After expiry, starting a new scan requires a one-time unlock; an active scan is not interrupted by expiry. Scanning works offline during the trial and with a cached unlock. Buying, restoring or verifying uses Google Play and requires a connection. Google processes purchase and associated account, payment and technical information under its privacy policy. We do not receive or store card details. Refunds cannot be authoritatively checked offline.

Limitations

The app detects magnetic-field changes, not wooden studs directly. It cannot guarantee that a wall is safe to drill, or reliably detect every metal, wire or pipe. Use an appropriate independent safety check before drilling.

Contact

For questions or requests about information under our control: post@bruketteknisk.no. Purchases can also be managed through Google Play. We will update this policy when data handling changes.
