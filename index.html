Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Android applications Angle Finder and Level.

No data collection

The apps do not collect, store, transmit, or share personal or device data.

On-device sensors

The apps use motion and orientation sensors locally on the device to calculate angles. Sensor readings never leave the device.

Permissions and third parties

The apps contain no advertising, analytics, account system, network access, or third-party tracking SDKs. They do not request location, camera, microphone, storage, or contact permissions.

Developer and privacy contact

These apps are published by EZNO Apps. For privacy questions, contact Post@bruketteknisk.no, the support address configured for Angle Finder in Google Play Console.

Opening this policy

The in-app privacy link opens this page in your chosen browser. Your browser and the website hosting provider may process ordinary connection information when you visit the page. The apps do not include sensor readings or other measurement data in the link.

Changes

If a future version changes these practices, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
