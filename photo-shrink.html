Simple Photo Shrink: Privacy Policy

Published by EZNO Apps. Effective 5 September 2026.

Simple Photo Shrink compresses photos locally on your Android device. We operate no server for this app and include no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. No account with us is required.

Photos and files

The app accesses photos you select or explicitly share with it. Photos are processed on your device, not uploaded to us. Your original is not modified. Copies that already fit the selected size can retain their original format and metadata, including location metadata. Recompressed JPEG copies do not copy camera or location metadata.

Private temporary files support processing and sharing. Files older than 24 hours are cleaned up on a later launch. Copies you explicitly save remain in the location you choose until you delete them. Sharing or saving to another app or cloud provider is your choice and is governed by that provider's practices.

Trial, purchases and access codes

The trial start time, cached purchase status and any complimentary access status are stored locally. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes this local state; saved photos outside app-private storage may remain. The app does not back up its private state through Android backup.

Google Play processes the optional one-time purchase and restoration. The app uses Google Play Billing to request product information and check and acknowledge purchases. Google processes purchase and associated technical information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card details. Purchase status can be cached for offline use. Restoring or verifying a purchase requires access to Google Play.

Permissions

The Android photo/file picker grants access to selected files. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or advertising-ID permissions. Network permissions support the Google Play Billing integration; photo compression itself works offline.

Contact and deletion

Contact post@bruketteknisk.no for privacy questions. If you email us, your email address and message are received to handle your request; please do not send sensitive photos. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations. For Google-held purchase information, use your Google account and Google's privacy controls.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change.
