Simple Alarm: Privacy Policy

Published by EZNO Apps. Effective 6 September 2026.

Simple Alarm processes motion locally on your Android phone. We operate no server receiving your alarm data. No account with us is required, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Motion, camera and alerts

The app reads accelerometer and gyroscope signals to detect movement. Camera permission is used to control the flashlight, not to record or upload photos or video. The app does not record microphone audio or location. Vibration and audio controls operate locally. Alarm sound temporarily raises the system alarm volume and restores it on normal stop; force termination or Android restrictions can affect restoration.

Local storage

Settings, alarm event history, trial start time and cached purchase entitlement are stored in private app storage. History can be cleared in the History screen. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes this local information and may reset the trial. Android backup is disabled for the app.

Purchases

The store version offers a 48-hour local trial and an optional permanent one-time unlock. No subscription or automatic charge. Expiry restricts starting a new alarm, not stopping an existing alarm.

Google Play processes purchases and restoration using your Play account. The app uses Google Play Billing to obtain product details and check and acknowledge purchases. Google processes purchase and associated technical information under its privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card details. Buying, restoring or verifying a purchase requires access to Google Play. Cached entitlement can work offline, but refunds cannot be authoritatively verified offline without a connection.

Safety and device limitations

Simple Alarm is not a monitored security service and does not guarantee protection against theft. Device restrictions, background operation, connected audio equipment and Do Not Disturb can affect alerts. Flashing light can affect people with photosensitive epilepsy. Avoid aiming the flashlight at people and keep the phone away from your ears when testing.

Contact and requests

Contact post@bruketteknisk.no with questions or requests about information under the developer's control. Purchases can also be managed through Google Play. We will update this page if the app's data handling changes.
